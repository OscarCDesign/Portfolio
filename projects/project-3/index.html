/ Overview

The Vision

The local university in Guadalajara (ITESO) needed a system that could integrate its multiple tools into a single platform. Key factors to consider included the university's well-established identity, its existing system architecture, and a history of previous attempts to create such a platform that had failed and been abandoned.

/ The Challenge

User Retention

It was clear from the start that a system designed the way it had been up to that point would fail. Something different was needed that could encourage usage and attract attention. To attend this, I implemented gamification through an Achievement and Reward system. As users complete both virtual and physical tasks, the university leverages the platform to improve its internal KPIs. Users earn achievements to personalize their experience and unlock rewards or discounts at on-campus stores. Consequently, the university benefits from more active participation in document updates, event attendance, group follow-ups, and service demand.

/ Results

Key Outcomes
